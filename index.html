Questions, answered.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy and on the Trust and compliance page.

Does LotusPond diagnose conditions?

No. LotusPond explains results; it does not diagnose. It shows each value next to its reference range with a short note in plain language.

A single result outside its range is common and is not a diagnosis. Talk to a doctor about what your results mean for you.

What files can I upload?

Lab report PDFs that contain text, CSV and JSON exports, DICOM files (.dcm) and photos of scans. Files are read in your browser, and every value is shown for you to check before it is saved. The original file is kept with your records.

Where are my records stored?

In the region that serves your country: India, Europe, the United States, or our international region. Sign-up shows the region before you agree, and the Trust page lists which countries go where.

Who can see my records?

Your records belong to your account, and every request for a profile’s data is checked against the account that is signed in. We do not sell health data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Can I keep records for my children or parents?

Yes. One account holds your own profile and one for each family member you add. Accounts are for adults, so a parent or guardian adds children as family profiles.

Which reference ranges does it use?

The range printed on your report, when there is one. Otherwise LotusPond shows a typical adult range and labels it as such, because labs use different methods.

How do I delete my data?

In Profile details you can export a profile as JSON, delete its records or remove the profile. In Account and sign-in you can delete your whole account with everything in it. The Privacy Policy explains how deletion works.
