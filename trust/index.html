1Where LotusPond Health is open

LotusPond Health is open to adults living in first. Other countries will open later; until then, sign-up tells anyone who picks one that LotusPond Health is not open there yet.

In a few places, local law requires health data to stay inside the country, or other restrictions apply. Those stay closed even when other countries open, and sign-up shows the reason below to anyone who picks one of them.

Partner-lab booking starts in India. Elsewhere, the lab finder shows public diagnostic labs, imaging centres and hospitals from OpenStreetMap, wherever they are listed.

2Storage by region

Each region is a separate database and file store, deployed in its own location. Sign-up picks the region from your country of residence and shows it to you before you agree. Where that region is outside your country, sign-up says so and asks for your agreement.

  • A small directory maps a keyed hash of each email address to its region, so sign-in knows where to look without keeping your email in readable form outside your region.
  • Your sign-in session remembers your region, so every later request goes straight there.
  • Data that is not about people, such as partner-lab details and cached public facility listings, is kept separately from records.

4Rules we apply everywhere

Some rules differ from country to country. We apply the strictest common ones to everyone:

  • Adults only for accounts. Anyone under 18 is added by a parent or guardian as a family profile.
  • Explicit, recorded consent. Accepting the Terms and Privacy Policy, consenting to the processing of health data and, where storage is abroad, agreeing to the region are separate steps. Each is stored with the text version, the time and your country.
  • No sale or sharing of health data, and no advertising trackers.
  • Data rights built in: export, correction and deletion are controls in the app that work straight away.
  • One cookie, and it is strictly necessary, so there is no cookie banner.

5Security measures

  • PasswordsHashed with Argon2id. We never see or store them in readable form.
  • SessionsRandom 256-bit tokens in an HttpOnly, HTTPS-only cookie. We store only a SHA-256 hash.
  • Account isolationEvery request for a profile’s data is checked against the signed-in account.
  • Cross-site protectionChanges are accepted only from our own pages.
  • Rate limitsRepeated sign-in and sign-up attempts are slowed by address and by email.
  • Content Security PolicyEach page may load code only from our own site. The public information pages run no scripts; the developer console runs only our own.
  • Safe file handlingUploaded files are served in a sandbox, and types a browser could run are only offered as downloads.
  • EncryptionHTTPS everywhere with HSTS, and encrypted storage for records and files in each region.
  • Audit logSecurity events on each account are recorded, which helps us investigate quickly.

7Breach readiness

Per-region storage and the account audit log let us find out quickly which records a problem touched. Our incident plan meets the strictest timelines that apply to us: notice to authorities within 72 hours under the GDPR and India’s DPDP Rules, and notice to affected people in the United States within 60 days under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule. People affected are told directly.

8Medical scope

LotusPond explains results; it does not diagnose. It is a personal health record that you keep for yourself and your family, and we offer it to you directly. Read the medical disclaimer in the Terms of Use.
