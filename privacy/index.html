The short version

  • We collect what you give us: your account details, the profiles you keep for yourself and your family, and the reports and scans you upload.
  • We use it to run LotusPond Health for you. We never sell it, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We process health data only with your explicit consent, which you give at sign-up.
  • Your records are stored in the region that serves your country: India, Europe, the United States or our international region.
  • You can export, correct or delete your data in the app at any time.
  • One strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in. There are no tracking cookies.
  • LotusPond explains results; it does not diagnose.

2Data we collect

Your account

Your name, email address and password. We store the password only as an Argon2id hash, so we cannot read it. We also keep your country of residence, your unit preferences and a record of each consent you give (which text, its version, the time and your country).

Profiles for you and your family

For each person you keep records for: name, relationship to you, date of birth, sex, height, weight, blood type, conditions, medications, allergies and notes. You choose what to fill in.

The sex in a profile also chooses which body the map shows, female or male, unless you pick the other body for that profile on your device. Your device then downloads that body’s pictures and 3D model from our servers. Like any request, these downloads can appear in the technical logs described below.

Health records

Lab results (marker, value, unit, reference range, date and source file), scans with their thumbnails, and the original files you upload, such as PDFs, CSV and JSON exports, DICOM files and images. Files are read in your browser. The values you confirm and the original file are then stored in your account.

Lab finder searches

The area, postcode or location you search with. Our server rounds a location to about 1 km before it looks up public listings, so your exact position is not sent to anyone else. Results for an area may be cached for up to 7 days, with no link to your account.

Security and technical data

A session cookie that keeps you signed in (we store only a hash of it, with your browser’s name and when it was last used), the network address of requests, used to limit repeated sign-in attempts, and a record of security events on your account, such as sign-ins, password changes and consents. Our servers keep short technical logs for security and troubleshooting for [Log retention period — to be completed by LotusPond].

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and we do not buy data about you from anyone. Everything we hold comes from you, from your browser when you use LotusPond, or, for facility listings, from OpenStreetMap, which is not data about you.

3How we use it

  • To provide LotusPond Health: store your records, show them on the body map, explain them with reference ranges and notes, keep your original files and find labs near you.
  • To keep accounts secure: sign-in, limits on repeated attempts, protection against abuse, and a record of security events.
  • To talk to you about your account, security, or changes to this policy and the Terms. We do not send marketing messages without your consent.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as answering rights requests, keeping consent records and notifying breaches.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use health data for advertising or to build marketing profiles. We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5Sharing

We do not sell personal data, including health data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We disclose data only in these cases:

  • Service providers that host our servers and storage in each region. They process data only on our instructions, under written contracts with confidentiality and security duties: [List of service providers and their locations — to be completed by LotusPond].
  • Lab finder look-ups. Our server sends OpenStreetMap services a rounded location or the area name you typed. Nothing that identifies you is sent.
  • Legal requirements, such as a valid court order. We will tell you unless the law prevents us.
  • A change of ownership. If LotusPond Health is merged or sold, your data stays protected by this policy, and we will tell you before any other policy applies to it.

We do not send your records to labs. When you book with a partner lab, you deal with the lab directly and its own privacy policy applies. When you open directions, your maps app (such as Google Maps or Apple Maps) receives the facility’s address and its own policy applies.

6Where your data is stored

Your country of residence decides where your records are kept. Each region is a separate database and file store. A small directory, kept apart from records, maps a keyed hash of your email address to your region so that sign-in knows where to look. It does not hold your email in readable form.

Transfers from the United Kingdom and Switzerland to our European region rely on those countries’ recognition of data protection in the EU. For the international region, which is located in [Location of the international region — to be completed by LotusPond], we use [Transfer safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses — to be completed by LotusPond] where your law requires them. 7Retention and deletion

  • Records: kept for as long as your account exists, and you decide what to keep.
  • Deleting: you can delete a file or scan, delete all records of a profile, remove a profile, or delete your whole account. Deletion removes the data from our live systems straight away.
  • Backups: deleted data leaves our encrypted backups within [Backup retention period — to be completed by LotusPond].
  • Sign-in sessions: a session lasts up to 30 days and is renewed while you use LotusPond. Signing out ends it, and expired sessions are removed.
  • Consent and security records: kept for [Retention period for consent and security records — to be completed by LotusPond] after an account is closed, so that we can show we met our legal duties.

9Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This section is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy under the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada Senate Bill 370 (2023) and similar state laws. It applies to consumer health data about people in those states.

Consumer health data we collect

Health conditions, medications, allergies and notes you add to a profile; body measurements such as height, weight and blood type; lab results and reference ranges; scans, images and the original medical files you upload; and lab finder searches, which could show that you are looking for health services.

Why we collect it

Only to provide the features you ask for: storing, showing and explaining your records, and finding labs near you.

Where it comes from

From you. Data about a family member comes from you as the person who keeps their profile.

Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We do not share consumer health data with third parties or affiliates, including LotusPond’s other products. The service providers that host our storage process it for us under contract and may not use it for anything else. We do not sell consumer health data; selling it would need your signed authorisation, and we do not ask for one.

Consent

We collect consumer health data only after your opt-in consent at sign-up. We would ask for separate consent before sharing it for any purpose other than providing the service. We do not set up geofences around health facilities.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect or share your consumer health data, and see it.
  • Get a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with, with their contact details. Today that list is empty.
  • Withdraw consent, and have your data deleted, including from our service providers’ systems.
  • Appeal if we decline a request.

How to use them

Use the controls in Profile details and Account and sign-in, or write to [Privacy contact email — to be completed by LotusPond]. We answer within 45 days. If we need longer, we will tell you why, and we may extend once by up to 45 more days. To appeal, reply to our decision within [Appeal window — to be completed by LotusPond]; we will answer the appeal in writing. If we decline it, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General or the Nevada Attorney General.

10Children and family profiles

  • LotusPond accounts are for adults aged 18 or over.
  • Children’s records are kept as family profiles added by a parent or guardian, who gives consent on the child’s behalf and manages the records.
  • When you add an adult family member, you confirm that they have agreed to you keeping their records.
  • We do not track children, monitor their behaviour, or show them advertising.
  • If we learn that someone under 18 has opened an account, we will close it and delete its data. Contact us if you think this has happened.

11Security

  • Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. Sign-in sessions use random 256-bit tokens, and we store only their SHA-256 hash.
  • The session cookie is HttpOnly, sent over HTTPS only, and limited to our own site (SameSite=Lax).
  • Every request for a profile’s data is checked against the account that is signed in.
  • Changes must come from our own pages; requests from other sites are refused.
  • Repeated sign-in attempts are limited by address and by email.
  • Our pages use a strict Content Security Policy. Uploaded files are served so that a browser cannot run them as part of our site.
  • Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and records and files are kept on encrypted storage in each region.

No system is perfectly secure. To report a security issue, write to [Security contact email — to be completed by LotusPond].

12Breach notification

If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you what happened, what it means for you, what we are doing about it and what you can do. Specifically:

  • EU, EEA and UK: we notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach, and tell you without undue delay when it is likely to put your rights at high risk.
  • India: we inform you and the Data Protection Board of India without delay, and send the Board a detailed report within 72 hours.
  • United States: we notify affected people without unreasonable delay and within 60 calendar days of discovery, and notify the Federal Trade Commission as its Health Breach Notification Rule requires, along with any state authority that state law names.
  • Elsewhere: we follow your local law, and we tell you directly in any case.
