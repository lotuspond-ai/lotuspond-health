1The anatomy models

LotusPond Health shows one of two reference bodies, female or male, picked from the sex in a profile (the female body when it is Intersex or not set). Both come from the Visible Human Project of the U.S. National Library of Medicine, which recorded the bodies of two people who donated them to science, as modelled organ by organ by the HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas.

The atlas does not include every structure LotusPond explains. The skull and teeth, the ribs, sternum and intervertebral discs, the bones of the arms, hands and feet, the stomach, oesophagus and diaphragm, the thyroid, parathyroid, pituitary and adrenal glands, the testes, the muscles of the female larynx, and the blood vessels of the head, neck and limbs come from Z-Anatomy and were fitted into each body.

Neither body is a scan of you. Your own organs differ in size, shape and position.

3What we changed

  • We combined the two sources. Z-Anatomy structures were moved, scaled and gently warped to fit each Visible Human body, using the bones and organs both sources share as guides.
  • We rebuilt the surfaces of some organs as single closed shapes, and removed parts that LotusPond does not show, such as reference points and lymph nodes.
  • We reduced the number of triangles so the models load quickly on phones, and grouped the structures into the organs LotusPond explains, with organ and tissue names in the files. The app colours each tissue when it draws them.
  • The body map pictures in the app, and the pictures of the body on this website, are renders of these models.
