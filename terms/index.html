1. Medical disclaimer

LotusPond explains results; it does not diagnose. LotusPond Health is a personal record and a way to understand your results. It is not a medical service, it does not give medical advice, and it is not a substitute for a doctor or other qualified health professional.

  • Explanations, notes, reference ranges and the 3D anatomy are general information. They are not a diagnosis or a treatment plan.
  • When your report prints a reference range, we show it. Otherwise we show a typical adult range, which may not suit children, pregnancy, older age or the method your lab used.
  • Reading a file can go wrong. You check every value before it is saved, and the original report from your lab is always the authoritative record.
  • We do not watch your results or alert anyone about them. A result outside its range is common and is not a diagnosis.
  • The 3D anatomy shows a reference body, female or male, built from real anatomical data. It is not a scan of you, and your own organs differ in size, shape and position.
  • Do not start, stop or change any treatment because of something you see in LotusPond without talking to a health professional.
  • Not for emergencies. If you feel unwell or think you may have a medical emergency, contact a doctor or your local emergency number straight away.
